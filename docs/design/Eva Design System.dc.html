07 · Voice
We say
“That can be common during this phase. Here is when it is worth contacting your provider.”
We don’t say
“You’re fine!” · “Don’t break your streak” · “Your fertility score dropped”
Eva describes, contextualizes and points to care. It never diagnoses, never reassures falsely, and never uses guilt, streaks or scores. Sensitive events stay neutral in language and in indicators.
